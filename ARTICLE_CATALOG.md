# Article Catalog — logam.taman.co.id

This lean catalog plans 96 non-geographic guides across 16 landscape-metal topics, grounded in the site's copper-and-brass craft and outdoor product routes. It supports, but does not duplicate, `/produk`, `/katalog`, `/cara-order`, or individual product pages. Structural, coating-life, electrical, water, lifting, hot-work, site-safety, standards, certification, warranty, and performance claims require authoritative references, project documents, system data, field evidence, and competent professional review.
