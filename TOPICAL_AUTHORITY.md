# Topical Authority — logam.taman.co.id

## Role and boundary

This domain should become a practical reference for designing, specifying, fabricating, installing, and maintaining metalwork in gardens and outdoor public or private spaces, anchored in the site's demonstrated copper-and-brass craft. It serves landscape owners, designers, contractors, facility teams, and buyers without duplicating product/order routes. Structural loads, foundations, anchors, wind stability, public-contact safety, electrical work, water-feature hygiene, lifting, hot work, chemical coatings, and compliance require project-specific calculations, site data, manufacturer systems, current authoritative requirements, and competent professionals; articles must not present generic dimensions or DIY procedures as approval.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub. LTM-01 briefs lead to material (LTM-02), structural (LTM-04), finish (LTM-07), and procurement (LTM-16) decisions. Product-system pages in LTM-09 through LTM-13 link to fabrication (LTM-05), interface (LTM-06), exposure (LTM-08), installation safety (LTM-14), and maintenance (LTM-15). Transactional links go only to relevant existing product, catalog, or order routes. Diagnostic pages connect failure, safe isolation, repair, and future prevention; no city name creates a new knowledge page.
